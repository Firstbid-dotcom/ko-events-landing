Our Story

How It Started

KO Events started the way most beautiful things do: small, personal, and for people we loved. It began with a kid's birthday party for a friend's daughter and a folding table that needed a little magic. Then came a baby shower for another friend, and another after that. Every setup got a little more detailed, a little more polished, and word started to spread.

What began as helping friends make their kids' birthday parties and baby showers feel special turned into a full-blown passion for design: the linens, the centerpieces, the tiny personalized touches that make guests stop and take a photo. Requests kept coming, and what started as a favor between friends grew into KO Events: Houston's go-to for stylish, elegant, and memorable table design.

Today we bring that same care and attention to detail to every event we touch, from intimate birthday celebrations to milestone-worthy affairs, one beautifully set table at a time.
